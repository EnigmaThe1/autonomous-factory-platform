# System-Wide Synthesis

## Purpose
Reconcile learning across the plugin's major adaptive layers into one coherent control surface.

## Use when
- several self-improvement layers have accumulated evidence
- their outputs may overlap or conflict
- the system should become more coherent without becoming rigid

## Procedure
1. Review compiler, orchestrator, tool-use, capability, and policy states.
2. Identify reinforcements, conflicts, and duplication.
3. Propose cross-layer synthesis adjustments.
4. Keep the result soft, adaptive, and autonomy-first.

## Output
- synthesis finding
- affected layers
- recommended adjustment
- supporting evidence
- confidence
