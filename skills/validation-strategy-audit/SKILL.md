# Validation Strategy Audit

## Purpose
Check whether the chosen validation strategy improved confidence efficiently.

## Procedure
1. Review the mission archetype and chosen validation route.
2. Evaluate whether checks were sufficient, excessive, or misaligned.
3. Detect missing proof, redundant proof, or high-value validation patterns.
4. Recommend validation-strategy improvements.

## Output
- validation strategy finding
- affected validation behavior
- suggested improvement
