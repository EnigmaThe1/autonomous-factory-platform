# Team Escalation Audit

## Purpose
Review whether multi-agent execution was justified and stayed bounded.

## Procedure
1. Inspect the mission graph.
2. Inspect escalation decisions.
3. Compare outputs to the original mission contract.
4. Check whether coordination overhead was justified.
5. Recommend whether to keep, reduce, or increase team usage.

## Output
- escalation assessment
- over-escalation risks
- under-escalation risks
- recommendation
