# Activation Lifecycle

## Purpose
Track and reason about optional capabilities through explicit lifecycle states.

## States
- discovered
- reviewed
- approved
- mirrored
- activated
- retired

## Procedure
1. Identify the capability and its current state.
2. Confirm why the mission may need it.
3. Check source trust, approval status, and policy.
4. Decide the smallest justified next state.
5. Record the request or transition.
6. Avoid activating anything that can stay merely approved or mirrored.

## Output
- capability id
- current state
- proposed next state
- justification
- approvals needed
