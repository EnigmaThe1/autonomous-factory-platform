# Staged Review Queue

## Purpose
Move newly discovered or refreshed capability intelligence into a clear review queue without skipping trust or approval boundaries.

## Procedure
1. Read the discovery feed.
2. Filter duplicates and low-value noise.
3. Group items by domain, source, and trust tier.
4. Add them to the review queue with clear reasons.
5. Keep approval and activation unchanged until separate review.

## Output
- queued items
- deduped items
- review priorities
