# Research-Layer Self-Improvement

## Purpose
Improve external discovery, source selection, freshness handling, and research-to-action handoff.

## Use when
- several missions have required research
- research quality can be compared against downstream outcomes
- the system should become better at doing the right amount of research

## Procedure
1. Review research behavior and source choices.
2. Compare research outputs with downstream execution quality.
3. Detect shallow, stale, noisy, or well-targeted research patterns.
4. Propose soft research-layer improvements.

## Output
- research finding
- recommended adjustment
- supporting evidence
- confidence
- rollback condition
