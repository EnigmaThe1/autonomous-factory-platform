# Intent Shaping Audit

## Purpose
Check whether early goal shaping helped or distorted mission formation.

## Procedure
1. Compare raw end-goal with resulting mission intent.
2. Compare mission intent with downstream execution quality.
3. Detect under-sharpening, over-sharpening, or misframing.
4. Recommend focused goal-formation adjustments.

## Output
- audit finding
- affected intent area
- suggested improvement
