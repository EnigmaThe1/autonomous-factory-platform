# Evidence-Weighted Promotion

## Purpose
Promote capabilities using the strength of evidence, not only phase timing.

## Use when
- a capability may help the mission
- phase signals are present but not sufficient on their own
- the system should avoid both premature staging and under-preparation

## Evidence channels
1. mission contract evidence
2. environment evidence
3. execution friction evidence
4. fallback weakness evidence
5. validation leverage evidence
6. source and trust evidence

## Output
- evidence strength
- decision
- why
- what would increase or decrease confidence
