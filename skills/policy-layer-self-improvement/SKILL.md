# Policy-Layer Self-Improvement

## Purpose
Improve the balance of the policy layer using mission outcomes and operator intent.

## Use when
- several missions have been executed
- the system has enough evidence to judge whether its soft policy guidance is useful
- the host-boundary posture should be checked without hardening ordinary work

## Procedure
1. Review policy-related outcomes and friction.
2. Review where trust posture helped or hindered.
3. Detect whether policy defaults are too weak, too strong, or well balanced.
4. Propose soft policy adjustments.

## Output
- policy finding
- recommended adjustment
- supporting evidence
- confidence
- rollback condition
