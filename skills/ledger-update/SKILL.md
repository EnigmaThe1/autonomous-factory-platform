# Ledger Update

## Purpose
Keep a lightweight project-independent record of important work.

## Use when
- a meaningful task has completed
- a plan was executed
- an investigation produced reusable findings

## Procedure
1. Record the task.
2. Record what changed, if anything.
3. Record what was validated.
4. Record risks or follow-ups.
5. Keep entries brief and factual.

## Output
- a single concise ledger entry
